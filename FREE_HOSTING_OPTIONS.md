### ✅ Why Koyeb:
- **Nano instance is free forever.**
- No credit card required to start.
- **No sleep/wake delays** (Always on).
- Auto-deploy from GitHub.
- Easy setup.

### 📊 Free Tier:
- 1 Nano Instance (Free)
- 512 MB RAM
- 2 GB Disk

Follow **`DEPLOY_KOYEB.md`**
